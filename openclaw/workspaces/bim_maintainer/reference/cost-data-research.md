## 1. Structural Steel

### Raw Material (Hot-Rolled)
| Period | Price/Ton |
|--------|-----------|
| July 2025 | $2,653 |
| October 2025 | $2,477 |
| January 2026 | $2,344 |
| HRC coil early 2026 | $950-$1,100/short ton |

Trend: Declining from 2025 peak. ~54% of industry professionals expect moderate gains through mid-2026.

### Fabricated Structural Steel (Material + Shop Labor)
| Complexity | Cost/Ton |
|-----------|----------|
| Basic fabrication (simple connections, standard shapes) | $1,000-$1,400 |
| Standard commercial (typical connections, coated) | $1,200-$2,400 |
| Complex (custom details, specialty coatings) | $2,400-$3,500 |
| High-complexity (seismic connections, galvanized) | $3,500-$4,200+ |

Fabrication rates: $60-$120/hour per welder or fitter. Coatings add $100-$400/ton.

### Steel Erection (Field Labor)
| Metric | Cost Range |
|--------|-----------|
| Per ton (erection only) | $400-$800 |
| Per ton (full install incl. material) | $1,500-$3,500 |
| Per SF (erection labor) | $3-$7 rural, $5-$15 urban |

Erection labor = 30-40% of total structural steel cost.

### Fabricated + Erected Total (In-Place)

|--------------|---------------------|

| Standard commercial | $3,500-$5,000 |

## 10. Estimating Steel Weight from Member Sizes

### AISC W-Shape Naming Convention
The designation **W[depth]x[weight]** directly encodes weight per linear foot.

Examples:
- W8x10 = 8" nominal depth, **10 lb/ft**
- W12x26 = 12" nominal depth, **26 lb/ft**
- W14x30 = 14" nominal depth, **30 lb/ft**
- W24x68 = 24" nominal depth, **68 lb/ft**
- W36x150 = 36" nominal depth, **150 lb/ft**

### Common W-Shapes and Weights (AISC 15th Edition)
| Shape | Weight (lb/ft) | Typical Use |
|-------|----------------|-------------|
| W8x10 | 10 | Light beams, wind girts |
| W10x12 | 12 | Light framing |
| W12x16 | 16 | Floor beams (light) |
| W12x26 | 26 | Floor beams (medium) |
| W14x22 | 22 | Light columns, beams |
| W14x30 | 30 | Beams |
| W14x48 | 48 | Columns (light) |
| W14x82 | 82 | Columns (medium) |
| W14x132 | 132 | Columns (heavy) |
| W16x36 | 36 | Floor beams |
| W18x35 | 35 | Floor beams |
| W21x44 | 44 | Floor beams |
| W24x55 | 55 | Girders |
| W24x68 | 68 | Girders |
| W27x84 | 84 | Girders (heavy) |
| W30x99 | 99 | Long-span girders |
| W33x118 | 118 | Long-span girders |
| W36x150 | 150 | Transfer beams |

### Other Steel Sections
| Shape | Typical Weights |
|-------|----------------|
| HSS (tube steel) columns | 20-65 lb/ft |
| C-channels | 5-30 lb/ft |
| Angles | 2-25 lb/ft |
| Steel deck (roof) | 2-4 PSF |
| Steel deck (floor, composite) | 3-6 PSF |
| Bar joists (open web) | 5-20 lb/ft |

### Weight Estimation Formula
```
Member weight (lb) = weight_per_foot (lb/ft) x length (ft)
Total steel (tons) = sum of all members (lb) / 2000
Steel density = 490 lb/ft3 = 0.2836 lb/in3
```

| Mid-rise office (4-8 story) | 12-18 PSF |
| High-rise office (8+ story) | 15-25 PSF |
| PEMB (clear span) | 3-6 PSF |

## 13. Market Outlook (2026)

- Steel prices declining from 2025 peaks but unlikely to drop significantly in 2026
- Labor costs rising 4-5% annually through 2026 (skilled trades shortage)
- Ready-mix concrete up 3-6% from 2025
- Transportation costs remain elevated
- Supply chain disruptions easing but not fully resolved
- Commercial construction door installation costs increased 23% over past two years
- Smart technology integration adds 15-25% to building systems
- Sustainability requirements increasing baseline costs 5-10%
